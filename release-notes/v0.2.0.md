# AI Router release v0.2.0

## Highlights

- A Fallback route switches after 5 cumulative attributable failures and switches back automatically once a higher-priority route recovers.
- Routes can exclude specific models from Fallback, with new route health status and a switch log.
- The Usage page adds "Today" and "Yesterday" filters.

## Fixes

- Fixed the menu bar activity animation being interrupted during local tool calls.
- Fixed background refresh making the menu route list scroll on its own.

## Notices

- No configuration migration is needed, and the app does not restart automatically after installation.
